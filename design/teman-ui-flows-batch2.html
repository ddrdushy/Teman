MOTION

What animation is allowed to do here

Motion is a feedback channel, not decoration. For an audience that includes people with slower processing speed and reduced contrast sensitivity, decorative movement is noise that competes with the thing they're trying to read. Four rules, and everything below obeys them.

Only in response to an action

A toggle flips, a sheet rises, a toast arrives, a tick draws. Nothing moves on its own, nothing loops for atmosphere, nothing animates on scroll inside the app. The one exception is the skeleton shimmer, which is communicating "still loading".

140–220 ms, ease-out, never linear

Fast enough not to delay, slow enough to be seen. Linear motion reads as mechanical; cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) is the single curve used everywhere. Nothing is ever lengthened to show it off.

Never blocks, never auto-advances

No screen moves on by itself. No modal animates in and out before it's read. An action's result is visible the moment it happens, with motion layered on top rather than gating it.

Reduced motion removes all of it

One media query kills every animation and transition in the product, and every screen still works. If a state is only legible because of the movement into it, that state is wrong.

Thirteen new components

Each of these was added because it appears on at least four screens. ListRow alone replaces bespoke markup on about twenty. Everything is live — toggle, press, expand.

16 · ListRow

The workhorse. Any navigable row: settings, people, circles, contacts. Whole row is the target at 60 px, chevron is decorative and never the only affordance.

17 · Switch

62×36 px — larger than the platform default because thumbs are imprecise. Carries ON/OFF text, so the state never rests on position and colour alone.

22 · Toast with undo

Beats a blocking confirm for reversible actions. Slides up 220 ms. Stays 8 seconds, not the usual 3 — a toast that vanished before it was read never existed. Persistent for screen readers.

23 · Skeleton

Grey shapes matching the eventual layout. The only looping animation in the product, because the loop is the message. Appears after 400 ms, never instantly — a flash of skeleton is worse than none.

24 · ProgressRing

Profile completeness. Fills over 700 ms when the screen opens — the one place a small reveal is justified, because progress is the message. Always paired with the number.

Community guidelines

Short version: be kind, be safe, and never ask anyone for money.

Money+
Teman is free. Never ask a member for payment, a loan, a donation or a tip. Never offer to pay for help. If anyone asks you for money, report it — this is the most common way people are taken advantage of.
Dating and romance+
Teman is not a dating platform. Romantic or sexual approaches are not allowed, in messages or in person.
Advice and medical help+
A Teman is a companion, not a nurse or a counsellor. Don't give medical advice or handle medication.
Passwords and codes+
Never ask for or share a PIN, password, OTP or banking detail — not even while helping with a phone.
Selling and recruiting+
No selling, no business recruiting, no religious or political persuasion.
