Brand & UI System — v1

Teman

No one should have to go alone.

The design foundation for a free, community companionship platform. Every colour, type and component decision below traces back to one of five product principles — human first, free companionship, dignity, safety before growth, simple enough for elderly users.

01

Brand strategy

Decided once, checked against constantly. If a future feature can't be justified against these, it doesn't ship.

Purpose

Teman exists so that no one in a neighbourhood has to face an appointment, an errand or an afternoon alone when someone nearby has time to spare.

Essence — 2 words

Quiet Presence

Not "care", not "help", not "service". Presence is the thing being offered, and quiet is how it should feel — a platform that gets people together and then gets out of the way.

Differentiator

Every comparable product sells a task being completed. Teman's unit of value is a person being beside someone — unpaid, unrated, unranked.

Narrative

Loneliness in a city is rarely a logistics problem. People can usually get to the hospital; what they can't do is walk in without anyone waiting for them to come out. Meanwhile the same neighbourhood is full of people with two free hours and no obvious way to give them away. Teman is the shortest possible bridge between those two facts — and its success is measured by how quickly people leave the app to meet in real life.

02

Four traits, each defined from both sides. The "never sounds like" column is the useful half — it's what settles arguments about button copy, error messages and notification tone later.

Say "Find a Teman"

Never "request a volunteer" or "get assistance". The person asking is a neighbour, not a case.

Never celebrate a streak

No badges for volume, no leaderboards, no "you're on fire". Kindness is not a score — Section 20 of the scope makes this a product rule, so it's a brand rule too.

03

Sisi — the signature motif

Di sisi is Malay for "by your side". Sisi is two presence forms standing together: you, solid — and the space beside you. That space is dashed and empty when no one has answered yet, and fills with amber the moment someone does. It is the logo, the request state, the notification icon and the completion badge, all one shape. The brand mark literally completes itself at the moment the product works.

04

Colour

Two directions were rejected deliberately. Clinical blue reads as a hospital system and makes the product feel medical, which Section 5 explicitly says it is not. Charity orange reads as "you are receiving aid" and breaks the dignity principle outright. Teal is calm and dependable without either association, and carries no religious coding — which matters on a platform serving Malay, Chinese and Indian communities in one feed.

05

Typography

Vollkorn for anything a person is being addressed by — names, requests, moments. It's a sturdy low-contrast humanist serif built for reading, not a fashion serif, so it stays legible at 72 and reads as a handwritten neighbourhood note rather than a software product. Source Sans 3 carries every functional surface: labels, buttons, forms, metadata. Both are open-source, which matters for a platform with no revenue, and both sit alongside Noto for Tamil and Chinese without a visual break.

Measure: 30–40 characters

Mobile body copy never runs edge to edge. Minimum 18 px side padding on every screen.

Two levels, not six

Headline and body. A caption is the practical third. Desktop H1–H6 ladders don't survive a phone.

Never below 15 px — one exception

No smaller text for legal copy, timestamps or disclaimers; if it doesn't fit at 15, cut words. The single exception is the bottom navigation label at 12 px, which is always paired with an icon and never carries information on its own.

08

The rules that keep it Teman

A design system holds under deadline pressure only if the prohibitions are specific. These are the ones that matter.

Amber never means anything but connection

Not a warning, not a highlight, not a decorative gradient, not an empty-state illustration. The instant amber appears somewhere without a person attached, it stops meaning anything.

No star ratings, ever

Trust is shown as verification status, count of completed Teman Moments, tenure, and descriptive words. A 4.6★ turns a neighbour into a gig worker.

Safety colour is never reused

Deep red belongs to SOS, reports and blocks alone. It never marks a delete button, a form error, or an overdue item.

No countdown timers or scarcity

"3 Temans left!" is conversion psychology aimed at people who are already anxious. Nothing in this product should create urgency it didn't find.

Two people on screen means Sisi is present

Any surface showing a match, a session or a completion carries the motif. Consistency is what turns a shape into a mark.

Everything is written to be read aloud

A caregiver will read these screens to a parent. If a sentence sounds strange spoken, it's wrong — and it has to survive translation into Malay, Tamil and Mandarin without losing warmth.
