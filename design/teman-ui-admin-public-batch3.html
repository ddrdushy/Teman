Teman — UI · Batch 3 of 3

Admin platform & public site

Two surfaces with opposite problems. Admin is a dense internal tool two or three people live in daily — it should look like an internal tool, and every hour spent styling it is an hour not spent on the member app. The public site is a conversion surface a stranger reads once. Neither follows the elderly rules; different audience, different constraints.

ADMIN

Design position

Desktop-first at 1280 px. Sidebar, dense tables, sticky headers, keyboard shortcuts on the verification queue because someone will sit in it for an hour during a recruitment drive. The one thing that must be right is that consequential actions are hard to do by accident and impossible to do without a log entry.

Two roles, enforced server-side

NGO coordinator — verification review, volunteer directory, circles, read-only analytics. Platform admin — everything, including suspension, roles and the audit log. The boundary is enforced in the API, not by hiding buttons.

Nothing destructive by accident

No bulk delete anywhere. Suspension needs a reason from a list and states exactly what the user will be shown. Every consequential action writes to audit_log, which is append-only — not even a platform admin can edit it.

Opening a record is itself an event

Viewing a user detail, revealing an ID document, or inspecting a match all get logged. The NGO will eventually ask who saw what, and "we don't log that" is not an acceptable answer for this product.

Queues, not dashboards

The home screen shows only counts that need a human decision, each linking to its queue. No vanity metrics, no charts nobody acts on. If a number doesn't imply an action, it belongs in analytics.

P4 · For volunteers ★The NGO's recruitment link. Write this page first.
🌐 EN

Two free hours is enough.

You don't need training, a car, or medical knowledge. You need to be willing to sit with someone.

What you'd actually do

Meet someone at a hospital entrance. Sit with them while they wait. Help find the right counter. Walk back out with them. Most of it is two to three hours.

What you wouldn't do

No medical care. No medication. No money, ever — in either direction. No commitment beyond the day you said yes to.

Before you start

Phone number, a photo, and a MyKad check reviewed by a person. About ten minutes.

Become a Teman
P5 · For familiesAnswers the real question: is my father safe with a stranger.
🌐 EN

You can't take the day off. Someone nearby can.

Set up a profile for your parent — they never need the app or even a smartphone. You create the request and you get every update.

Is a stranger safe?

Every Teman is identity-checked against a MyKad by a person. You see their record before you accept, and you always choose. Your parent's address stays hidden until you both agree.

You'll know what's happening

A message when they meet, when the session starts, and when it ends safely. A trusted contact can get the same updates.

Language matters

P7 · SafetyLong, plain, unmarketed. Reading slightly legal here builds trust.
🌐 EN

How safety works

Teman is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger or needs medical help now, call 999.

Identity

MyKad or passport, checked against a selfie by a person. Documents are deleted 90 days after review; we keep the outcome, never the number.

Before you meet

Both people must accept. Only the general area is shown until then. A trusted contact can be told where and when.

If something goes wrong

A safety button during every session, and reports read by a person. A serious report stops matching immediately, before anyone reviews it.

P10 · PrivacyPlain language first, formal text below. PDPA.
🌐 EN

Privacy, in plain words

The formal policy is below. This is what it means.

Your ID

Reviewed by one person, deleted after 90 days. We never store the number itself, only a scrambled version used to spot duplicates.

Your address

Hidden until you and a Teman both accept. Every time it's revealed, we record who saw it — and you can ask us for that list.

Your location

Only while a session is running, only if you turn it on, and only while the app is open. We can't track you in the background and we won't pretend otherwise.

Deleting your account

Your profile goes. Safety records stay, without your name, because they protect other people.

Remaining four pages follow the same structure: P3 For people who need a Teman — problem-aware, real situations in their own words, and what Teman is not. P8 About — the narrative from the brand kit, no founder-hero framing. P11 Terms — including the liability position and the not-an-emergency-service statement. P12 Contact — a real route to a person with a stated response time, because visible contact information is itself a trust signal.
