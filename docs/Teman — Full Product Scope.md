# Teman — Full Product Scope

**Tagline:** *No one should have to go alone.*

## 1. Product Vision

**Teman** is a free, community-based companionship and support platform that connects people who need someone with people who are willing and available to be there.

The problem Teman solves is not simply transportation or task completion.

Sometimes a person can physically travel to a hospital, bank, shop, or appointment — but they **do not want to face it alone**.

Sometimes an elderly parent needs someone beside them.

Sometimes someone living alone needs another person to accompany them to an appointment.

Sometimes a person simply wants someone nearby to sit with them, walk with them, or listen.

At the same time, many people in the same community have spare time and genuinely want to help.

Teman connects these two sides.

> **I need someone.**  
> **I can be there.**

Teman is **not a paid companion marketplace**.

There are no subscription plans, paid companions, commissions, bidding systems, premium matching, or user-to-user payments.

The core relationship is based on **community, trust, kindness and availability.**

---

# 2. Core Product Principles

Every Teman feature should follow five principles.

### Human first

The product should feel like asking someone in your community for help, not booking a service provider.

### Free companionship

A Teman is not hired.

People offer their presence and time voluntarily.

### Dignity

People requesting help should never feel like they are receiving charity.

The language should be:

**“Find a Teman”**

rather than:

**“Ask a volunteer for assistance.”**

### Safety before growth

Because Teman involves strangers meeting in real life, identity, trust, privacy and safeguarding are core product features.

### Simple enough for elderly users

The app should have large buttons, simple language and very few steps for common actions.

# 3. Primary User Groups

## A. Person Needing a Teman

Someone who needs companionship or practical assistance.

Examples:

- Elderly person
- Person living alone
- Person attending hospital alone
- Person recovering from illness
- Person unfamiliar with an area
- Someone who needs emotional companionship
- Someone who needs help completing an everyday activity

---

## B. Family Member / Caregiver

Someone creating and managing requests on behalf of another person.

Example:

> “My father has a hospital appointment Tuesday at 10 AM. I cannot take leave from work. I need someone to accompany him.”

They can:

- Create requests
- Manage the person's profile
- Review potential Temans
- Track active companionship
- Receive check-in notifications
- Contact the assigned Teman

---

## C. Teman / Community Helper

A person willing to give some of their time.

Example:

> “I'm free this Saturday morning around Cheras. If anyone nearby needs help going to a clinic or running an errand, I can help.”

A Teman may:

- Browse nearby requests
- Declare availability
- Offer assistance
- Join someone going to the same destination
- Become a recurring companion

---

## D. Community / Organisation Coordinator

Later, verified organisations can participate.

Examples:

- Community centres
- Resident associations
- NGOs
- Universities
- Religious/community organisations
- Senior-care organisations

They can organise trusted community members and support people within their area.

---

# 4. Main Product Modes

The home screen should revolve around **three primary actions**.

## 1. I Need a Teman

Create a companionship request.

Example:

**Hospital Visit**

> Kuala Lumpur Hospital  
> Friday, 9:30 AM  
> Need someone to accompany my father during his appointment.

---

## 2. I Can Be a Teman

Tell the community that you are available.

Example:

> Available today  
> 2 PM – 6 PM  
> Bangsar / Brickfields  
> Can accompany hospital visits, grocery trips or walks.

Teman can then recommend nearby requests matching that availability.

---

## 3. I'm Going There Too

People travelling to similar destinations at similar times can find each other.

Example:

User A:

> Going to Hospital Kuala Lumpur  
> Thursday 10 AM.

User B:

> Need someone to accompany me to Hospital Kuala Lumpur  
> Thursday 9:30 AM.

Teman can identify the overlap.

> **You're both heading to HKL around the same time.**

Both users can choose whether they want to connect.

# 8. Emotional Companionship

This should be positioned carefully.

Teman provides **human companionship, not therapy**.

Someone may create a request such as:

> “I've been alone most of today. Would like someone to have coffee and talk.”

Or:

> “Having a difficult week. I don't need advice. Just someone willing to listen.”

Request types:

- Someone to talk to
- Someone to listen
- Coffee and conversation
- Sit together
- Walk and talk
- Quiet company
- Spend some time together

Users should be able to indicate:

**What would help me today?**

- Just listen
- Casual conversation
- Walk together
- Coffee / meal
- Quiet company
- Advice is okay
- Please don't give advice

---

# 9. Social Companionship

Not every request has to originate from a problem.

Examples:

- Walk in the park
- Morning walk
- Coffee
- Lunch
- Visit a community event
- Go to a market
- Visit a library
- Attend a local activity

Teman remains explicitly **non-dating**.

Romantic solicitation and dating behaviour should not be part of the platform.

---

# 10. Welfare Visit / Check-In

Family members can request someone to visit a loved one.

Example:

> “My mother lives alone in PJ. I'm travelling this weekend. Could someone from the community visit her Saturday afternoon and have tea with her?”

The requester specifies:

- Person being visited
- Relationship
- Preferred time
- Purpose
- Mobility/accessibility considerations

Sensitive address information is disclosed only after an appropriate match and consent process.

---

# 11. Digital Assistance

Especially useful for elderly users.

Requests can include:

- Help using smartphone
- Install/use an app
- Online government service
- Online booking
- Video-call setup
- E-wallet/basic digital assistance
- Online forms
- Device settings

Financial passwords, PINs, OTPs and banking credentials should never be shared with Temans.

# 12. Request Creation

Request creation should be extremely simple.

### Step 1 — What do you need?

Choose a category.

### Step 2 — Who needs the Teman?

- Me
- Parent
- Relative
- Someone I care for

### Step 3 — Where?

Approximate location initially.

### Step 4 — When?

- Now / today
- Specific date
- Specific time
- Flexible

### Step 5 — What kind of help?

Short description.

### Step 6 — Preferences

Optional:

- Preferred gender
- Language
- Age range
- Accessibility requirements
- Driving required / not required
- Same destination preferred
- Verified Temans only

### Step 7 — Publish

Nearby suitable Temans can discover or receive the request.

# 13. Availability Mode

Users should not need to wait for requests.

They can proactively say:

> **I'm available.**

Fields:

- Date
- Start time
- End time
- Area
- Distance willing to travel
- Types of support
- Transportation availability
- Languages
- In-person preferences

Example:

> **Available Today**  
> 3 PM–7 PM  
> Petaling Jaya  
> Hospital companion • groceries • walking • conversation

The matching engine can surface relevant requests.

---

# 14. Matching System

Matching should consider more than distance.

Core matching factors:

### Location

How close are the two users?

### Time

Do their available schedules overlap?

### Request type

Can the Teman provide the requested assistance?

### Language

Can both comfortably communicate?

### Preferences

Gender, accessibility, age or other preferences.

### Trust level

Identity verification and community history.

### Previous connection

Users may prefer someone they have previously met successfully.

### Same destination

Higher relevance when both people are already going to the same place.

---

# 15. Match Experience

Once a suitable Teman is identified:

> **Azlan may be able to accompany your father.**

Show only relevant trust information initially:

- First name
- Profile photo
- Languages
- General area
- Verification badge
- Teman history
- Community feedback
- Shared interests where appropriate

Both sides must accept.

Only after mutual acceptance should additional coordination details become available.

---

- Mobility/transport options

> “Retired teacher living in Bangsar. Usually free weekday mornings. Happy to accompany seniors to clinics, grocery shopping or just have coffee and chat.”

# 17. Person Needing Support Profile

Possible fields:

- Preferred name
- Age group
- Languages

- Family/caregiver connection

> **People I Care For**

**Dad — Siva**

- Age: 72

# 19. Trust & Verification

Teman's trust system should have multiple levels.

## Basic Verification

- Mobile number
- Email

## Identity Verified

Government ID verification.

For Malaysia:

- MyKad
- Passport for foreigners

ID information should not be publicly visible.

## Selfie / Liveness

Ensure the account belongs to the verified individual.

## Community Verified

Potential later layer through:

- Organisation
- University
- NGO
- Resident association

## Enhanced Verification

Optional higher-trust status, potentially including appropriate background checks where legally feasible.

---

# 20. Trust Indicators

Instead of a single star rating, display meaningful trust information.

Example:

**Identity Verified ✓**

**12 Teman Moments**

**Member for 8 months**

Feedback:

- Kind
- Patient
- Reliable
- Good listener
- Helpful
- Respectful

Avoid turning kindness into a gig-economy performance score.

---

# 21. Safety System

Safety must exist before the first real-world match.

## Before Meeting

- Identity verification
- Mutual acceptance
- Approximate location initially
- Clear meeting details
- Share meeting with trusted contact
- Community guidelines

## During Meeting

When the companionship starts:

> **Start Teman**

Both users confirm.

The system records:

- Start time
- Meeting context
- Expected duration

Optional live location sharing can be enabled with trusted contacts.

## End Meeting

Both users select:

> **End Teman**

Then provide private safety feedback.

---

# 22. Trusted Contact

Each user should be encouraged to register at least one trusted contact.

Before a companionship:

> “Dushy is meeting Amir at HKL between approximately 10 AM and 12 PM.”

Trusted contacts can receive key status updates where the user has enabled them.

---

# 23. SOS / Safety Assistance

During an active Teman session, an easily accessible safety button should be available.

Possible actions:

- Contact trusted person
- Call emergency services
- Share location
- Report unsafe interaction

Emergency functionality should use local emergency service pathways appropriate to Malaysia.

---

# 24. Privacy

Privacy should be intentionally conservative.

Do not initially expose:

- Exact home address
- Phone number
- Full ID number
- Private family information
- Detailed medical information

Connections occur inside Teman first.

Information is revealed gradually and only when necessary.

---

# 25. Messaging

Teman needs basic coordination messaging for accepted matches.

Purpose:

- Confirm meeting point
- Clarify assistance
- Inform arrival
- Coordinate changes

It should **not become a general social-chat platform**.

Communication remains tied to companionship requests and relationships.

---

# 26. Recurring Teman

Some connections naturally become recurring.

Example:

> Every Wednesday  
> 7:30 AM  
> Morning walk with Uncle Ravi.

After successful companionships, users may propose:

**Make this recurring**

Possible frequency:

- Weekly
- Every two weeks
- Monthly
- Custom schedule

Both parties must agree.

---

# 27. Favourite / Trusted Temans

A user may build a small trusted circle.

Example:

### Mum's Temans

- Farah

- Kumar

When a new request is created, the family may first ask existing trusted Temans before opening the request to the wider community.

**Condo Residents**

**University Volunteers**

**Senior Support Circle**

**Sri Lankan Community KL**

Requests may be:

- Private to circle
- Shared with selected circles
- Public nearby

# 29. Location-Based Community Discovery

The app can show:

> **Around You**

Examples:

**2 people need a Teman today**

**5 Temans available nearby**

**1 hospital visit matches your schedule**

Exact locations should not be visible before matching.

---

# 30. Request Urgency

Requests can be categorised as:

### Planned

Example: appointment next Thursday.

### Today

Help needed sometime today.

### Soon

Help needed within the next few hours.

Teman should **not position itself as an emergency service**.

True emergencies should be redirected appropriately.

---

# 31. Transportation

Teman itself is **not a ride-hailing service**.

A Teman may indicate:

- I have a car
- I can drive
- I use public transport
- I can accompany you in your transport
- Meet at destination

If someone offers transport, this should be treated as part of voluntary companionship rather than a paid ride.

No fares or transportation fees should be processed through Teman.

---

# 32. Accessibility

Profiles and requests can indicate accessibility needs.

Examples:

- Wheelchair user
- Slow walking pace
- Need frequent rest
- Hearing difficulty
- Vision assistance
- Cannot climb stairs
- Needs accessible transport

Potential Temans see these requirements before accepting.

After each Teman interaction:

### How was your experience?

Simple descriptors:

- Kind
- Respectful
- Patient
- Reliable
- Helpful
- Good listener

Private questions:

**Did you feel safe?**

Yes / No

**Would you meet this person again?**

Yes / No

# 36. Reporting & Blocking

Users can:

- Block someone
- Report behaviour
- Report harassment
- Report inappropriate romantic/sexual behaviour
- Report money requests
- Report fraud
- Report unsafe driving
- Report impersonation
- Report discrimination
- Report abuse

Serious reports should immediately restrict matching while reviewed.

# 37. Behaviour Rules

Teman should clearly prohibit:

- Dating solicitation
- Sexual behaviour
- Harassment
- Selling services
- Asking for payment
- Loans or money requests
- MLM/recruitment
- Religious proselytising
- Political campaigning
- Exploiting vulnerable users
- Requesting OTPs/passwords
- Medical impersonation
- Professional medical advice without credentials

---

# 38. Emotional-Support Safeguards

Temans are companions, not counsellors.

The app should clearly state:

> **Teman offers companionship and community support. It is not a substitute for professional mental-health or emergency services.**

Companions should be encouraged to:

- Listen
- Be respectful
- Avoid diagnosing
- Avoid prescribing solutions
- Avoid making promises they cannot keep

If a conversation indicates immediate danger, the product should guide users toward appropriate professional/emergency support.

# 44. Product Analytics

Because Teman has no monetisation, success should not be measured using revenue.

Primary metric:

> **Successful Teman Moments**

A Teman Moment means a requested companionship was successfully completed.

Supporting metrics:

- Requests created
- Requests matched
- Match completion rate
- Median time to match
- Repeat companions
- Recurring companionships
- Active Temans
- People helped
- Family-managed requests
- Unfulfilled requests
- Safety incident rate
- Community retention

The goal is not:

> “How long can we keep users inside the app?”

The goal is:

> **“How quickly can we get people safely off the app and together in the real world?”**

# 45. Geographic Model

Teman should begin hyperlocal.

Hierarchy:

**Country → State → City → Area → Community**

Example:

Malaysia  
→ Kuala Lumpur  
→ Brickfields  
→ Local community

A hyperlocal model increases the likelihood that someone genuinely nearby can help.

# 47. Product Roles

The complete platform therefore has these roles:

### Individual

Can request and provide companionship.

### Caregiver

Can manage another person's requests.

### Teman

Provides companionship.

A single person may hold all three roles.

### Community Coordinator

Manages a verified community.

### Organisation Admin

Manages an organisation's Temans.

### Platform Moderator

Handles safety and moderation.

### Platform Administrator

Manages the overall platform.

# 48. What Teman Is NOT

Defining this is important.

Teman is not:

- Grab
- Ambulance service
- Home nursing
- Healthcare provider
- Therapy platform
- Dating app
- Maid service
- Task marketplace
- Freelance platform
- Paid caregiving marketplace
- Delivery service
- Social-media network
- Emergency service

Teman provides one thing:

> **Human presence and everyday companionship.**

**Session completed.**

# 54. Long-Term Product Vision

Over time, a user should be able to open Teman anywhere in their neighbourhood and feel:

> **There are people around me I can rely on.**

And another user should be able to think:

> **I have two free hours today. Maybe someone nearby could use them.**

The strongest version of Teman is not an app people compulsively use every day.

It is a **trusted community infrastructure** sitting quietly in the background until someone needs another human being.
